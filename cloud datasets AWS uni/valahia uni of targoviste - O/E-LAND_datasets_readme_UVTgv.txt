1. Abstract for the dataset 
The data set contains the following files:

- Electrical and heat demand of the ABR building from the University Campus. Horuly profiles for the years 2019 and 2020.
- Electrical and heat demand of the C building from the University Campus. Horuly profiles for the years 2019 and 2020.
- Electrical and heat demand of the ICSTM building from the University Campus. Horuly profiles for the years 2019 and 2020.

- PV electrical generation of PV park n1 in UVTgv.Hourly profile for the year 2019.
- PV electrical generation of PV park n2 in UVTgv.Hourly profile for the year 2019.
- PV electrical generation of PV park n3 in UVTgv.Hourly profile for the year 2019.
- Solar Thermal capacity factor of solar colectorin UVTgv.Hourly profile for the year 2021.
- Wind generation of windturbine in UVTgv.Hourly profile for the year 2021.

*Generation profiles are in kWh
