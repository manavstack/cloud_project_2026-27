PROPOSED SOLUTION

From experiment to campus forecast

  1. Refresh the inputs. IIT Bombay observations end in June 2018 and Valahia records end in December 2020; current operational predictions require a recent, regularly updated feed.
  2. Resolve data quality explicitly. Review zero readings and off-cadence gaps with source documentation; do not silently interpolate missing consumption.
  3. Expand only on compatible contracts. Validate La Trobe's unit and timezone; keep Valahia heat separate from electricity; compare source experts and the MoE only after cadence, horizon, and target units align.
